Applying your selection

Save and restart Homebridge to apply changes.

Excluding a robot removes its Homebridge accessory and automation helpers, not the robot from Dreamehome. Adding it again may require pairing and restoring its room and automations.

Pairing with Apple Home

  1. After saving and restarting, open Plugins and find Dreame Vacuum Matter.
  2. Open the three-dot menu, choose External Accessories, then select your vacuum.
  3. In Apple Home, choose Add Accessory and scan or enter that robot's Matter code. Repeat for each robot.

Use each robot's own code, not the main bridge code. HAP is only needed for optional automation helpers.
